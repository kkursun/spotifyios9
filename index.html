Spotify Remote

A lightweight remote control, built for old Safari.

Set your Spotify Client ID first: open this file, find SPOTIFY_CLIENT_ID near the top of the <script> block, and paste in the Client ID from your Spotify Developer Dashboard app. Also add this exact page URL as a Redirect URI in that app's settings.

Connect your Spotify account to control playback on any device that's already signed in (phone, computer, speaker). Requires Spotify Premium.

Can't log in on this device? Log in on a computer, tap "Show link" under "Transfer login" there, and open that link here. Or paste the token below.
